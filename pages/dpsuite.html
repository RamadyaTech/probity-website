Privacy compliance that runs itself

DPSuite gives privacy and DPO teams the workflows regulators expect — consent captured and honored, data-subject requests fulfilled on time, processing activities documented, and breaches handled to the clock. Because it shares data with Probity GRC, your privacy controls and risks live alongside the rest of your GRC program.

Consent & Preference Center

Capture, store, and honor consent and communication preferences across web, app, and offline channels — with a full, timestamped audit trail.

Data Subject Requests

DSAR / data-principal request intake, identity verification, and SLA-tracked fulfillment for access, correction, erasure, and portability.

Data Mapping & RoPA

Maintain Records of Processing Activities and visualize data flows across systems, vendors, and jurisdictions — kept current, not rebuilt yearly.

DPIA & Assessments

Guided Data Protection & Privacy Impact Assessments with risk scoring, mitigations, and sign-off — triggered when high-risk processing changes.

Breach Management

Log incidents, assess notifiability, and drive 72-hour regulatory notification workflows with roles, timelines, and a defensible record.

Retention & Disposal

Policy-driven retention schedules with defensible deletion — so personal data doesn't outlive its lawful purpose, and you can prove it.

Built for India's DPDP Act

DPSuite maps directly to the obligations that matter under India's DPDP Act — from consent and data-principal rights to breach notification and grievance redressal.
